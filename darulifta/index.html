What Is a Darul Ifta?

A Darul Ifta (دار الإفتاء) is an institution where qualified Islamic jurists — known as Muftis — issue formal religious rulings called fatwas. These rulings provide Muslims with authentic, evidence-based guidance on matters of worship, daily life, business, and personal conduct according to Islamic law (Sharī'ah).

The Darul Ifta at Dars-e-Nizami draws on centuries of traditional scholarship, ensuring every answer is grounded in the Quran, authentic Hadith, and the writings of classical scholars across the four major schools of Islamic jurisprudence.

Seeking Knowledge Is an Obligation

The Prophet ﷺ said: "Seeking knowledge is an obligation upon every Muslim." (Ibn Mājah). Our Darul Ifta exists to make authentic Islamic knowledge accessible to every Muslim, regardless of where they live. Whether you are in London, Toronto, Berlin, Sydney, or Karachi — you deserve clear, reliable answers to your religious questions.

Every fatwa issued through this portal is prepared with scholarly rigour and pastoral sensitivity, balancing textual evidence with an awareness of the questioner's circumstances. We do not give casual opinions; we give considered, referenced answers you can rely upon.
