Dars-e-Nizami course and admission FAQs

Clear answers to common questions about our online courses. For individual placement or teacher availability, contact admissions.

Getting started

What is Dars-e-Nizami?

Dars-e-Nizami (also written Darse Nizami or Dars Nizami) is the classical madrasa curriculum of South Asia. It teaches Arabic grammar, Quran translation and Tafsir, Hadith, Fiqh and their principles step by step, leading to the Alim qualification for men and the Alimah qualification for women. See the course overview and the year-by-year syllabus.

Dars-e-Nizami kitne saal ka hota hai? (How many years is it?)

Brothers study the Alim / Baneen course for eight years. Sisters study the Alimah / Banat course for six years. Your starting year is confirmed during admission, based on any previous study.

Is the online course free?

No. There is a one-time admission fee and monthly tuition. The admission fee for your region is Checking fee…. Ask admissions about any scholarship or instalment arrangement before you pay.

Is the course for women?

Yes. Sisters can join the six-year online Alimah course and choose group or one-to-one classes. You can ask for a female teacher, and admissions confirms a suitable arrangement. Read the Alimah course details.

Do I need to know Arabic before I start?

No prior Arabic or Islamic knowledge is required. The institute assesses your level during admission and places you in a suitable year.

How do I attend the classes?

All classes are online. After your enrolment is confirmed, your student LMS dashboard shows your class times, joining links, lessons, books and assignments.

Who teaches the classes?

Teachers assigned by the institute guide your lessons, assignments and assessment. Ask admissions about the teacher and teaching language for your class before you enrol. Sisters can request a female teacher.

Fees, admission and certification

How long is the online Dars-e-Nizami course?

The Alim / Baneen programme for brothers is eight years. The Alimah / Banat programme for sisters is six years. Entry year and placement are confirmed during admission.

Can sisters study online?

Yes. Sisters can apply for the six-year Alimah programme and choose group or one-to-one study. Mention any female-teacher preference and your preferred class time so admissions can confirm availability.

How do I register?

Create an account in the student LMS, complete the admission form, follow email verification instructions and submit the admission payment evidence. Your dashboard shows application, payment and enrolment progress.

Can I study from the UK, USA or Canada?

Yes, classes are online. Provide your city, country and preferred local class time. The team confirms a suitable teacher and schedule; daylight-saving changes can affect the time difference with Pakistan.

Is this a Wifaqul Madaris recognised qualification?

The public curriculum page references Wifaqul Madaris syllabus documents. Recognition, affiliation, external examination eligibility and certification must be confirmed directly with the institute for your programme; a syllabus reference is not proof of recognition.

Are books and recordings available?

The public book library and LMS provide access to available study resources. Ask the teaching team about required editions, book availability and whether recordings are provided for your selected class.

How can I contact a teacher or admissions?

Use the institute’s WhatsApp contact link or email info@darsenizami.net. Include your course, country, preferred time and question. Avoid sending sensitive identity documents through the public chat assistant.
