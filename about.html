About Dars-e-Nizami Online Institute

We provide online Islamic learning for brothers and sisters, with structured study pathways and a student learning management system.

Our programmes

Alim course for brothers

An eight-year Baneen pathway, from Arabic foundations to advanced Islamic studies.

Alimah course for sisters

A six-year Banat pathway, with group and one-to-one study options.

Quran and short courses

Explore Qaida, Nazra, Tajweed, Hifz, Arabic and introductory Islamic studies.

How we support students

Students can submit an admission application, choose a course for their group, request a suitable class time and follow their progress through the LMS. Teachers guide study, assignments and assessment.

Before you enrol

Ask the team to confirm the teacher, teaching language, class schedule, tuition and assessment or certification arrangements for your selected programme. We publish syllabus references to help you understand the course content.

Our institute operates online. Contact us through WhatsApp or info@darsenizami.net.
