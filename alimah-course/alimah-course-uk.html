Online Alimah course for sisters in United Kingdom

Sisters in United Kingdom can apply for our six-year online Alimah / Banat programme, with group or one-to-one study.

Arrange classes around your local time

Use Europe/London when describing your time zone. Pakistan is five hours ahead of GMT and four hours ahead of British Summer Time. UK clocks change seasonally; confirm whether your class follows local time or Pakistan time.

When applying, include London, Birmingham, Manchester or another UK city, your preferred days and local class time. These are preferences; admissions confirms teacher and batch availability. Ask about the teaching language and any female-teacher preference before enrolling.

Tuition and admission fee

Checking your fee region…

Monthly Alimah tuition is Checking fee… for group classes or Checking fee… for one-to-one classes. A separate Checking fee… admission fee applies once. Fees are displayed for your visiting region. International applicants should confirm a suitable payment method with admissions; transfer charges may vary.

What will you study?

The programme covers Arabic grammar (Sarf and Nahw), Quran translation and Tafsir, Hadith, Fiqh, Usul al-Fiqh and Islamic beliefs. Subjects and books vary by year and by the Baneen or Banat pathway.

See the year-by-year Wifaqul Madaris syllabus tables and source PDFs, or visit the online book library.

The published syllabus is a curriculum reference. It does not by itself establish institute affiliation, board registration, examination eligibility, accreditation or certificate equivalence. Ask admissions to confirm the arrangements applicable to you.

How to apply online

  1. Open the student LMS and choose Create account. Enter your contact details, gender, city and country.
  2. Complete the admission application, select an available course for your group and give your preferred class time. Supply any required identification securely in the LMS.
  3. Follow the email verification instructions after submitting your application.
  4. Pay the admission amount displayed in the payment step and submit payment evidence. NayaPay ID: QAZIJEE@NAYAPAY. International applicants should confirm a usable payment method with admissions.
  5. Sign in to your dashboard to follow application and payment progress. Payment verification and enrolment status determine access to course learning materials.

Do not send identification or payment evidence to a public page or chat widget. Use the secure LMS application flow.
