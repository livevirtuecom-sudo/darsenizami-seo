Editorial approach and corrections

Our public pages help students understand online Islamic study, compare learning pathways and prepare for admission. They should give useful information without promising an outcome that the institute has not confirmed.

Types of information

  • Course pages describe the institute’s available pathways. Teacher assignment, class availability and individual admission arrangements are confirmed by the admissions team.
  • Study guides offer practical learning suggestions. Sample timetables and exercises are examples, not compulsory course requirements or guarantees of progress.
  • Syllabus tables identify their source documents and editions. A syllabus reference does not establish board affiliation, examination eligibility or certificate equivalence.
  • Religious questions may depend on personal circumstances. General articles and automated answers are not substitutes for an individual answer from an appropriate teacher or scholar.

Sources and attribution

Where a page reproduces or summarizes a syllabus, follow its source PDF link and check the edition. We distinguish the institute’s programme information from third-party resource titles. External links are provided for context and do not establish ownership, endorsement or permission to redistribute content.

Automated assistance

The site includes an AI assistant. Automated answers can make mistakes, miss context or refer to information that has changed. Confirm sensitive matters, fee disputes, certification and individual religious rulings with the institute. The presence of an article or AI answer is not evidence that a named scholar reviewed it.

Corrections

To report an inaccurate course detail, broken link, source mismatch or unclear explanation, email info@darsenizami.net. Include the page URL, the wording at issue and, where possible, a supporting source. The team can assess the correction using that information.

Advertising and independence

Applying for or displaying an advertising service does not mean that service approves the institute’s teaching, qualifications or advice. If advertising is enabled, an advertisement is a third-party promotion, separate from the institute’s educational guidance. Do not treat an ad as a course requirement or a book-download control.
