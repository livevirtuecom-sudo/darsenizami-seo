Online Dars-e-Nizami courses for brothers and sisters

Study Dars-e-Nizami (also written Darse Nizami) online: classical Islamic subjects through an eight-year Alim pathway for brothers or a six-year Alimah pathway for sisters. Explore the syllabus and apply through our student LMS.

Quran and short courses

Explore Qaida, Nazra, Tajweed, Hifz, Arabic and introductory Islamic studies.

What will you study?

The programme covers Arabic grammar (Sarf and Nahw), Quran translation and Tafsir, Hadith, Fiqh, Usul al-Fiqh and Islamic beliefs. Subjects and books vary by year and by the Baneen or Banat pathway.

See the year-by-year Wifaqul Madaris syllabus tables and source PDFs, or visit the online book library.

The published syllabus is a curriculum reference. It does not by itself establish institute affiliation, board registration, examination eligibility, accreditation or certificate equivalence. Ask admissions to confirm the arrangements applicable to you.
