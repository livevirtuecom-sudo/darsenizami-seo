How to apply online

  1. Open the student LMS and choose Create account. Enter your contact details, gender, city and country.
  2. Complete the admission application, select an available course for your group and give your preferred class time. Supply any required identification securely in the LMS.
  3. Follow the email verification instructions after submitting your application.
  4. Pay the admission amount displayed in the payment step and submit payment evidence. NayaPay ID: QAZIJEE@NAYAPAY. International applicants should confirm a usable payment method with admissions.
  5. Sign in to your dashboard to follow application and payment progress. Payment verification and enrolment status determine access to course learning materials.

Do not send identification or payment evidence to a public page or chat widget. Use the secure LMS application flow.
