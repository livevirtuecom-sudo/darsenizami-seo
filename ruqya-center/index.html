What Is Ruqya?

Authentic Quranic Healing for Spiritual Ailments

Ruqya Shariah is the practice of reciting verses of the Holy Quran and authentic duas of the Sunnah over a person, water, or oil, seeking Allah's protection and cure from spiritual harm. It is used for conditions commonly described as sihr (black magic), jinn possession or influence, evil eye (nazar), and waswas (intrusive whispers).

Amaliyat is a related but distinct practice — a structured course of Quranic recitation and dhikr, prescribed by a qualified scholar for a specific concern such as a marriage problem, business or rizq blockage, family dispute, or protection from harm, performed consistently over a set period under guidance.

Frequently Asked

Ruqya & Amaliyat — Common Questions

What is Ruqya and how does it work?
Ruqya Shariah is reciting Quranic verses and authentic Sunnah duas over a person, water, or oil to seek Allah's protection and cure from sihr, jinn influence, evil eye and waswas — performed strictly within Quran and Sunnah, free of shirk.
What is Amaliyat in Islam?
Amaliyat is a structured course of Quranic recitation, dua, and dhikr prescribed by a scholar for a specific concern — marriage, rizq, business, or protection — performed consistently over a set period under guidance.
Can Ruqya treatment be done online via WhatsApp or video call?
Yes — diagnosis and ongoing guidance are conducted through WhatsApp voice notes, calls and video, so clients anywhere in the world can be guided from home.
How do I know if I am affected by black magic or jinn possession?
Reported signs include unexplained fear, recurring nightmares, sudden aversion to Quran recitation, and persistent marital, business or health issues with no clear cause. A proper assessment with a qualified scholar is recommended over self-diagnosis.
Is Ruqya effective for evil eye and waswas?
Ruqya verses for evil eye and waswas are well established in the Sunnah and widely used for protection and relief. Ruqya is a spiritual practice and is not a substitute for medical or psychological treatment where one is needed.
How long does a Ruqya or Amaliyat program take?
It depends on the case. Some clients notice relief within a short course, while longstanding cases may need a structured program over several weeks with regular WhatsApp follow-up.
Do you provide Ruqya services internationally?
Yes — our online Ruqya and Amaliyat service is available to Muslims wherever they live, entirely via WhatsApp.
Is your Ruqya center Shariah-compliant?
Yes. All guidance is supervised by qualified Islamic scholars and based strictly on Quran and authentic Sunnah, free from shirk, superstition, or unlawful practice.
